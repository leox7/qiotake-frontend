Simple steps

How it works

  1. 1

    Search

    Browse homes near KCA. Filter by area, room type and your monthly budget.

  2. 2

    Request

    Choose your move-in and move-out dates and send a booking request. It costs nothing, and the landlord reviews it.

  3. 3

    Pay

    Once the landlord confirms your booking, pay your deposit with M-Pesa on your phone. You pay only after the landlord has said yes.
